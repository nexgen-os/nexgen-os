# Code of conduct

Nexgen OS is built by people who describe places for a living and by people
who write software. Both are welcome, whatever their country, language,
employer, background or level of experience, and the project expects of
everyone, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions:

- **Respect.** Disagree with an idea, never with a person. No insults, no
  harassment, no discrimination, no unwelcome attention, in public or in
  private.
- **Care for the facts.** A claim about a standard, a source, a licence or a
  figure comes with where it was read. A mistake is corrected, not defended.
- **Care for the reader.** Write for someone who was not there: say what
  changed, why, and how to check it. Everything is in English; local names
  and vocabulary labels keep their language.
- **Care for the users.** What is merged runs on databases nobody here
  controls: a released migration is never edited, a breaking change is
  announced and written down.

Behaviour that falls short is reported to the maintainers privately, through
GitHub's private reporting on this repository (*Security → Report a
vulnerability*, which opens an advisory only the maintainers read) or to any
maintainer directly; a report is read by a maintainer who is not involved in
the matter, kept confidential, and answered. The maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and comments that do not follow these rules, and may
exclude, temporarily or permanently, anyone who breaks them.

This text draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1, shortened to what this project needs.
