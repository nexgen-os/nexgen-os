Nexgen OS

An open foundation for describing the built world — sites, buildings, roofs, meters, participants, devices, sensors — in 3D and over time, so that architecture, energy, operations and compliance tools share one model.

Status: 0.0 — the core schema, the architecture, weather and solar modules, the Home Assistant, geocoding and CityJSON libraries, the Open-Meteo and PVGIS connectors, a first country pack and seven example datasets. Tested; nothing is stable until 0.1.

The pact

  • The data is yours: it sits in a Postgres database you control, in tables you can read, and leaves only when you say so.
  • The words are plain: the best of the trades' standards — IFC, CityGML, Brick, SensorThings, QUDT — with names anyone can understand, the standard term written next to each.
  • The rules are data: what a country, a grid operator or a market decides is a pack of files anyone can read, propose and prove.

What it is, and what it is not

Nexgen OS is the open-source foundation and reference language, under AGPL-3.0 for the core and the modules and MIT for the format libraries and the connectors. Nexgen, the commercial platform of WeSmart, is built on it and remains WeSmart's product. The foundation is also made for research: a base that universities and research centres can start from, test a method on and publish a module for.
